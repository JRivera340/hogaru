# Script — Hogaru Product Strategy Brief (~7 min)

## SLIDE 1 — The business (45 sec)
Hogaru is a home-services marketplace: cleaning, carpentry, electricity, gardening. Everything booked online, with verified professionals. Our customers are busy people, families, couples, seniors — anyone who needs help at home and has neither the time nor trusted contacts.

## SLIDE 2 — The problem (1 min)
The problem is simple: people have no time for the house, and a day is never enough. Today there's no single reliable place to find help. And when they do hire, the service often isn't what they imagined — trust is low no matter the ratings. Competition is one outdated app with bad reviews. That confirms demand, but it also shows this category punishes bad management.

## SLIDE 3 — The value (45 sec)
Our pitch: we help busy people by giving them home services with easy, fast booking and verified pros — so they get their time back. Three pillars: everything in one place, frictionless booking, verified trust.

## SLIDE 4 — Your vision (30 sec)
We want to be the most trusted home-services provider. And trusted, for us, means three concrete things: rigorous verification, honest reviews, and clear prices.

## SLIDE 5 — MVP (2 min)
To validate, not to impress: a simple app to browse pre-verified employees and book in a few taps. Cleaning only, one zone. It covers search, booking with date and time, payment, ratings, and a minimal admin view. Deliberately out: open pro registration, dynamic pricing, more categories, advanced filters. In 8 to 10 weeks we want 60 bookings, 4-out-of-5 satisfaction, and 30% rebooking. And the rule is clear: if they don't come back, we fix trust before adding categories.

## SLIDE 6 — Stakeholders (30 sec)
Clients and families, Hogaru, background checkers, the payment provider, and the professionals — with hand-onboarded supply: no open marketplace until trust is proven.

## SLIDE 7 — How you will measure success (45 sec)
The goal: the most trusted platform for hiring home services. 500 active users, 150 completed bookings, 4-out-of-5 satisfaction. If that happens, customers win back hours, trust beats the incumbent, and rebooking proves the loop. Thanks — happy to take questions.
