## 2. Hogaru — Problem Framing / Value / Vision / MVP / Stakeholders / OKR (45 ítems)

### 1 · Problem Framing
- **What is the problem?** Busy people do not have time to do household chores.
- **Why should we solve this problem?**
  - Because customers don't have a centralized way to find someone to do their household chores
  - Because customers are not comfortable with the services independently the score professionals have
  - Because this app has a better potential that it actually has, because of the bad management
- **What evidence supports it?**
  - There's only one app that does the same thing, and it's outdated
  - Reviews of a similar app show dissatisfaction with the service provided

### 2 · Value Proposition
> We help **busy people** who **don't have time to do household chores** by **providing home services through a digital experience with easy and quick booking and trusted service providers** so they can **have more free time to do their activities**.

### 3 · Product Vision
> Our Vision is **become the most trusted provider of home services solutions**.

### 4 · MVP
- **MVP:** A simple app that lets you list employees and make reservations.
- **What do we want to learn?** To understand what customers are truly interested in and identify which parts of the booking process or employee views need the most optimization.

### 5 · Stakeholder
Clients / Families · Hogaru · Background Checkers · Payment Provider · Digital Product

### 6 · OKR
- **Objective:** Become the most trusted platform for hiring home services and childcare.
- **KR1:** Acquire 500 active users looking for home services.
- **KR2:** Reach 150 completed job bookings through the app.
- **KR3:** Obtain an average user satisfaction score of at least 4 out of 5 for ease of booking and trust in the listed service providers.

## 3. Peer Review (14 ítems)
*"Your mission is to help them build a better product."*

- **What did you like?** We liked that people are able to look for a job in the home care world or either offer a job that they need to get done. Also, that the app can connect people with trusted home professionals.
- **What is still unclear?** Even though we think it's a good idea we are not sure how the marketplace would work in practice for both sides. For example, how professionals would join and be verified, how prices would be defined, and how HogarU would manage service quality across very different categories such as plumbing, gardening or repairs. We would also want to understand how the new marketplace would connect with the existing HogarU ecosystem without making the experience more fragmented.
- **Would you choose the same opportunity? Why?** Yes, but we would validate it before expanding too broadly. The opportunity makes sense because HogarU already has experience, users and trust in home services, so expanding into adjacent categories is a logical step. However, we would first test a few high-demand services and validate whether users really want to solve those needs through HogarU before building a large marketplace.
